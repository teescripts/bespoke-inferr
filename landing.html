Why local AI?

Four things a hosted API cannot offer, no matter the price.

Prompts, documents and results stay on your machine. There is no third-party service in the loop to trust — or to breach.

Predictable cost

Always available

Local models keep working on planes, on locked-down networks, and on the day an API provider has a bad one.

Your hardware, your rules

Why we built it

A workaround that became a product

Every local-AI setup eventually meets the same constraint: the model you want is a little larger than the machine in front of you. This project began exactly there — a 16 GB Apple Silicon Mac running LM Studio capably, until the natural step toward larger models presented itself: a 32 GB Intel Mac, with twice the memory and, in theory, twice the headroom.

The RAM was there; the software was not. LM Studio does not run on Intel Macs, and the available alternatives either did not fit the workflow or did not fit the machine. Rather than leave capable hardware idle, we built the missing piece ourselves — a model manager that runs on any Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel alike, and, as the idea proved itself, on Windows as well.

That workaround is now Bespoke Inferr — open source, free, and shaped entirely by the constraints that inspired it.

F.A.Q

Questions & answers

What is Bespoke Inferr?

A local interface for managing Ollama models on your computer. It shows what you have installed, lets you start and stop models, search Hugging Face for new ones, and download them — all in one place, without touching a command line.

How do I start it?

Just launch the app. It checks that Ollama is installed and running, starts it if needed, then opens the interface — there's nothing to set up by hand.

Does it work offline?

The parts that talk to your installed models work offline. Searching and downloading obviously need a connection; everything local keeps working without one.

Can I chat with my models in the app?

Yes — the Chat tab streams replies from any installed model. Pick one from the dropdown, type, and the conversation stays in the session until you start a new one.

What does the Log tab record?

Every chat turn — model used, tokens in and out, generation time — plus model starts and stops. It's stored locally in the app's own storage; nothing is sent anywhere, and you can clear it in one click.

Which size should I download?

The suitability badge on each model is a quick guide: smaller quantizations run on less RAM, larger ones keep more quality. The app compares each option against your machine.

Can I use a server on another machine?

Yes — the connection settings let you point the app at a remote Ollama host instead of the local one.

Does this app phone home or collect data?

No. It talks to Ollama and to the model registries you search — nothing else. Your preferences stay on your machine.

How do I remove a model I no longer need?

Each installed model card has a remove button with a confirmation — the model and its files are deleted from Ollama.

Bespoke Inferr costs nothing and always will. Support is optional — there are three independent ways to contribute, and each funds something specific.
