the project, so users always know which build is the real one.

## What you can do

- Run, study, modify, and fork the code for any purpose
- Distribute your version **under a different name**, with the MIT
  license and copyright notice attached
- Redistribute unmodified official binaries as long as the license and
  third-party notices travel with them
- Truthfully describe compatibility: "a fork of Bespoke Inferr" or
  "compatible with Bespoke Inferr"
- Refer to the project nominatively in articles, comparisons, and reviews

## What you cannot do

- Distribute modified builds under the "Bespoke Inferr" name
- Use the name or logo to promote, advertise, or endorse your version
- Imply that this project, its maintainers, or its contributors are
  affiliated with, sponsoring, or approving your product
- Sell or otherwise monetize "Bespoke Inferr"-branded builds

## Logo and brand imagery

Screenshots, logos, wordmarks, and site brand assets (including
everything in `images/` used as branding) are **not** covered by the
MIT grant and may only be used with the unmodified official project.
